Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

At TinyGenius, we respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you use our website. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to information, cookies and third-party services.

We do not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to use our calculators.

Our website hosting provider or other technical service providers may process technical information, such as browser details, IP addresses and server logs, as part of operating and securing the website.

Information processed in connection with the website may be used to:

  • Identify technical problems and protect website security.
  • Understand website usage where analytics services are enabled.

3. Cookies and Analytics

TinyGenius does not currently require cookies for calculator functionality. Our hosting provider or future third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies.

If analytics or advertising services are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. Third-party service providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

TinyGenius is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information processed about you.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services and legal obligations evolve. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through our website contact page once it becomes available.
