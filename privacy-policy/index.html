Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

At TinyGenius, we respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you use our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and the choices available to visitors.

1. Information We Collect

TinyGenius provides online calculators and digital tools. The values you enter into our calculators are processed by the calculator functionality in your browser. We do not intentionally transmit these calculator inputs to Google Analytics.

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use our calculators.

Our hosting provider and technical service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information and server logs to operate, maintain and secure the website.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with the website may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our website.
  • Improve the performance and usability of our tools.
  • Identify technical problems and protect website security.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor engagement.
  • Evaluate which tools and pages are useful to visitors.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use TinyGenius.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, interactions with website content, browser and device information, referral sources, approximate geographic information and usage data.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to distinguish visits and measure website activity. This information helps us understand traffic patterns and improve our website and tools.

Analytics information is processed by Google on our behalf in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. Depending on Google's processing arrangements, information may be processed in countries outside your country of residence.

We do not intentionally configure Google Analytics to collect information that directly identifies you or the values entered into our calculators.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website measurement functionality.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TinyGenius calculators do not require cookies to perform their basic calculations. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies for website measurement.

Our hosting provider and other technical service providers may also use technologies necessary to operate and secure the website.

5. Third-Party Services

TinyGenius is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics for website measurement. These providers may process technical information in accordance with their respective privacy policies and service terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

TinyGenius is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or object to the processing of personal information relating to you. You may contact us to raise a privacy-related request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services and legal obligations evolve. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
