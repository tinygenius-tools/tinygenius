Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Welcome to TinyGenius. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you visit and use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to visitors.

1. Information We Collect

TinyGenius provides free online calculators and digital tools. You can use our calculators without creating an account or submitting personal information.

Values entered into our calculators are processed by the calculator functionality in your browser. We do not intentionally collect or store those calculator inputs on our servers.

When you visit our website, technical information may be processed by our hosting provider and analytics services. This may include browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and general usage information.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with TinyGenius may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our website.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behaviour.
  • Improve our calculators and user experience.
  • Identify technical errors and performance issues.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security threats.

3. Cookies and Google Analytics

TinyGenius uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website.

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, browser information, device information and traffic sources.

We use this information to measure website traffic, understand which tools are useful and improve our website.

Google may process information collected through Google Analytics in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy .

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

4. Third-Party Services

TinyGenius uses third-party services to operate and understand the performance of the website.

  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting and delivery.
  • Google Analytics: Website traffic measurement and usage analysis.

These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell visitors' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate, maintain and improve TinyGenius.

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. International Visitors

TinyGenius is accessible to visitors from different countries. Depending on the services used and your location, information may be processed in countries other than your own.

We aim to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

7. Children's Privacy

TinyGenius is intended for a general audience. Our website is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information relating to you.

You may also be able to manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services and legal obligations evolve. Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated revision date.
