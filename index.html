About TinyGenius

TinyGenius builds practical tools that help independent professionals make better commercial decisions. We help freelancers and creators understand the value of their work, assess the terms attached to a deal and prepare to negotiate with confidence.

Our calculators turn important numbers into clear starting points. Fair & Just takes the next step: helping you examine offers, alternatives, scope, usage rights and possible responses before you agree.

Our philosophy

TinyGenius Negotiates for Fair and Just Value of Work. We believe people should be able to understand the trade-offs in a deal—not just accept a number they are given.
