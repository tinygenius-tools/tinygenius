What is a YouTube revenue calculator?

A YouTube revenue calculator helps creators estimate their potential advertising earnings based on video views and revenue per thousand views (RPM).

Whether you are a new YouTuber, an established creator or a video publisher, understanding estimated revenue can help you plan your content business.

How is YouTube revenue calculated?

This calculator uses the following formula:

Estimated revenue = (Monthly views ÷ 1,000) × RPM

RPM means Revenue Per Mille. It represents the revenue a creator receives per 1,000 video views after YouTube's revenue share, as reported in YouTube Analytics.

What factors affect YouTube earnings?

  • Audience location and demographics.
  • Video category and subject matter.
  • Advertiser demand and seasonal trends.
  • Video length and advertising opportunities.
  • Monetized playbacks and viewer engagement.
  • Differences between Shorts and long-form content.

What is a good YouTube RPM?

There is no universal RPM. It varies considerably depending on audience, content category, geography, video format and advertiser demand.

Use your actual RPM from YouTube Analytics whenever possible. Any assumed RPM should be treated as a scenario, not a guaranteed earning rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does YouTube pay for 1,000 views?

Earnings vary according to RPM, content type, audience location and monetization. Enter your own estimated RPM to calculate potential revenue.

What does RPM mean on YouTube?

RPM means Revenue Per Mille. It estimates how much a creator earns per 1,000 video views after YouTube's revenue share.

Does this calculator guarantee YouTube earnings?

No. It provides an estimate based on your inputs. Actual earnings can be different and are not guaranteed.

Does the currency selector convert exchange rates?

No. It changes the display currency only. Enter your RPM in the currency you select.
