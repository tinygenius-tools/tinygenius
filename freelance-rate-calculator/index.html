What is a freelance rate calculator?

A freelance rate calculator helps independent professionals estimate how much they should charge clients for their work. It considers your desired income, business expenses and the number of hours you can realistically bill.

Whether you are a graphic designer, writer, developer, consultant or digital marketer, knowing your minimum hourly rate can help you price your services more confidently.

How is your freelance hourly rate calculated?

The calculator uses a simple formula:

Hourly rate = (Desired annual income + Annual expenses) ÷ Annual billable hours

Annual billable hours are calculated by multiplying your billable hours per week by your working weeks per year.

Why should freelancers calculate their rates?

  • Set prices that account for business expenses.
  • Plan income goals more realistically.
  • Avoid undercharging for professional services.
  • Understand how available working hours affect pricing.
  • Build a more sustainable freelance business.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge as a freelancer?

Your rate depends on your desired income, business expenses, experience, skills and available billable hours. Use this calculator to estimate your minimum starting rate.

What are billable hours?

Billable hours are the hours you can directly charge clients for. Administrative work, marketing and business development may take additional time.

Should I include business expenses in my rate?

Yes. Software, equipment, internet, professional services and other business costs should be considered when setting your freelance prices.

Does this calculator convert currencies?

No. The currency selector changes the currency format only. Enter your income and expenses in the currency you select.
