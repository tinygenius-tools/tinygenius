What is UGC pricing?

User-Generated Content (UGC) is content created by individuals for brands to use in marketing. UGC creators may produce product demonstrations, testimonials, unboxing videos and short-form advertisements.

How is the price calculated?

Your suggested quote is the sum of your video creation fee, editing fee, usage rights, additional revisions and raw footage charges.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a large following to become a UGC creator?

No. UGC creators are generally paid for producing content, not necessarily for their follower count.

What are usage rights?

Usage rights describe how and where a brand can use your content, including whether it can use it in paid advertisements.

Is this a guaranteed market rate?

No. This calculator provides an estimate based on the fees you enter. Actual prices depend on experience, content complexity, licensing terms and client budgets.
