What is UGC pricing?

User-Generated Content (UGC) is content created by individuals for brands to use in marketing. UGC creators may produce product demonstrations, testimonials, unboxing videos and short-form advertisements.

How is the UGC quote calculated?

Your estimated quote is the sum of the five amounts you enter: video creation, editing, usage rights or licensing, additional revisions, and raw footage. Enter 0 for any component that does not apply.

Important: the calculator is an itemised total, not a market-rate estimator. It does not decide how much a licence, exclusivity restriction or paid-ad permission is worth. Set those amounts after agreeing the deliverables, platforms, duration, territory and permitted use with the brand.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a large following to become a UGC creator?

No. UGC creators are generally paid for producing content, not necessarily for their follower count.

What are usage rights?

Usage rights describe how and where a brand can use your content. Confirm platforms, organic or paid use, licence duration, territory, creator-handle advertising, editing permissions and renewal terms. See our UGC usage rights pricing guide.

Does this calculator automatically price paid usage or exclusivity?

No. Enter the licensing fee you decide is appropriate for the specific permissions requested. The calculator simply adds the amounts you provide.

What should I do if a brand wants a lower price?

Consider adjusting the scope rather than discounting the same package automatically. You could discuss fewer deliverables, shorter licence duration, fewer platforms or no raw footage. Use Fair & Just to prepare a reasoned counteroffer.

Is this a guaranteed market rate?

No. This calculator provides an estimate based on the fees you enter. Actual prices depend on experience, content complexity, licensing terms and client budgets.
